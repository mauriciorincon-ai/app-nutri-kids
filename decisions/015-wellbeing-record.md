# ADR-015 — Wellbeing record: how they are, how they come to the table, what they accept, their habits (it records here; what repeats is counted by ADR-021, never interpreted as a cause)

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Sprint:** S010 · **Revises:** ADR-013 (new write kinds in `may()`, new change-log kind `wellbeing`) and ADR-011 (the day record carries four more maps) · **Owner decisions:** G-Plan S10 «1a» (a parent picks the age range; the date of birth is never stored).

## Context

Today a refusal only leaves the «rejected» chip on a meal note (`day-log.ts`): nobody knows if it was taste, tiredness or lack of hunger, how many times the food was offered, or how the night before went. Four VISION roadmap items fix that: **symptoms and wellbeing**, **emotional traffic light before eating**, **acceptance log** and **healthy habits**. They touch hard rules 2 (no calories, weight, BMI, no blame), 3 (never diagnose or prescribe) and 6 (a minor's health data never leaves the phone in clear). It is also the raw material of the S14 radar, which is out of scope here: this sprint only writes things down.

## Decision

1. **One record, no new record types.** Everything rides in the existing `day:<date>` record, which is already end-to-end encrypted, stamped per entry, signed per person and merged by newest stamp. It gains four optional maps (a day saved before S10 reads the same and merges without loss; the storage key stays `nutrikids.daylog.v3`): `moods` (`meal:<id>`), `food` (`<meal>|<food>`, each part URI-encoded), `well` (the eight symptoms and a short note) and `habits` (`sleep`, `screen`, `activity`, `sun`; minutes). The server still sees only «plan» or «item» and ciphertext: a test pushes sentinels through the wire and finds none of the kinds, names or contents.
2. **Closed vocabulary, plain words, no scale.** Mood: happy, tired, anxious, not hungry, other. Symptoms: energy, sleep, mood, bowel movements, pain, nausea, reactions, hunger/fullness, each with 3–5 options in everyday words and one 140-character note. Nothing is scored, summed, ranked or compared across symptoms, and the app never reads a symptom as a sign of anything. A «reaction» other than «none» only offers a link to the family's emergency plan (S9: whom to call, which card to show); it never says what to do.
3. **Acceptance is derived, not stored.** Each entry says what the child did with a food in a meal (looked, touched, tasted: all three count as an exposure), how it went (loved, accepted, did not accept, or not said yet), which texture and presentation worked, and whether the food is new. The exposure count is computed by counting entries across the days, so there is no counter that two phones can desync. The copy says it often takes offering a food many times, with no pressure and no fixed number (USDA NESR 2019 for 0–24 months, moderate evidence; NESR 2025 for 2–6 years).
4. **Age range, not date of birth (owner decision 1a).** A parent picks one of 1, 2, 3–4, 5, 6–12, 13–17 years (the cut points of the AASM and WHO tables) in the Profile; it lives in the encrypted `profile` record as a stamped field. Once a year (365 days after it was chosen or confirmed, with the date injected, never the clock) the app asks whether it is still the same range; confirming re-stamps it. A date of birth is never stored.
5. **Reference figures: only these, each with its source in the data** (`src/lib/wellbeing/references.ts`; a test fails if a figure has no source; source citations are written in each language; the WHO 2019 page numbers could not be verified, so only its section is cited). Sleep, AASM 2016 (Paruthi et al., _J Clin Sleep Med_ 12(6):785-786; endorsed by the AAP): 1–2 years 11–14 h with naps · 3–5 years 10–13 h with naps · 6–12 years 9–12 h · 13–18 years 8–10 h. Activity and screen under 5, WHO 2019: 1–4 years at least 180 min of activity at any intensity (3–4 years: at least 60 of those moderate to vigorous) · screen: 1 year not recommended, 2–4 years up to 1 h, «less is better». Activity 5–17, WHO 2020 (read in the official PDF, p. 25): an average of at least 60 min a day of moderate to vigorous activity across the week. Screen 5–17, WHO 2020 (p. 29): «limit… particularly recreational screen time», **no figure**. Water: the goal of the plan itself (`hydration.glassesPerDay`), the app brings no other. Sun and nature: no official figure, so it is recorded and the screen says so. A habit below or above its reference is shown with symbol, text and colour («within what is recommended» / «below the reference» / «above the reference», never red, never «failed»; activity from age 5 is a weekly average, so a single day is only noted, and up to age 5 the sleep figure is the 24-hour total with naps) plus «a reference is not a verdict; if something worries you, ask the pediatrician».
6. **Who writes what** (`may()` in `roster.ts`; four new write kinds, plus `age`): parent everything · caregiver mood, acceptance, how-they-are and habits (a default of the builder: the person at home logs screen and sleep) · school only the mood and acceptance of the meals in its time slot · nutritionist reads and leaves notes (the existing `day-note`), nothing else. The age range is the parent's alone. Every received entry must come from a member allowed that kind, with a valid signature, a valid key and value for its map and a stamp no more than 5 minutes ahead; anything else is dropped and counted. Each map has a cap, and a test shows a very full day still fits in one 64 KB record. The change log gets one kind, `wellbeing` (which day and which map, never the content). Food keys must be canonical (`foodKey(meal, food)`), so one meal cannot count twice. When a day is over a map cap, the cut is deterministic (tombstones first, then the oldest stamp) so phones converge.
7. **Texts live apart.** The wellbeing copy is `src/i18n/wellbeing.ts` ({es, en}, written in each language, parity test), loaded only by the screens that show it, so the shared bundle does not grow (script budget stays 440 KB). Every string passes `looksLikeMedicalAdvice` in a test, and a second test rejects blame and alarm words. Note: that function had no runtime caller before this sprint, so the copy is guarded by tests, not at runtime.

8. **Look.** Ten `--mood-*` tokens (five hues plus surfaces, light and `.dark`) in `globals.css`, away from the traffic light and never red; every mood and status is symbol + word + tone. Recorded in `design-system.md`.

## Consequences

- The day record grows with the wellbeing maps; the existing «too big for what is shared» warning covers the (tested, remote) case of overflow.
- Older phones that do not know the new maps would drop them on re-push: the family moves to this version together (no mixed versions are supported before the H-2 close).
- The S14 radar can read these records without a migration: the shape is the final one.
